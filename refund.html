Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Before Placing an Order

Customers are encouraged to check the product description, photographs, price, availability and delivery information before confirming an order.

2. Return Requests

Return requests will be reviewed based on the product condition and the reason for the request. Please contact us as soon as possible if there is an issue with your order.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Product

If you receive a product that appears damaged, defective or different from the confirmed order, please contact us promptly and provide clear photographs or video evidence of the issue.

4. Evidence for an Issue

For damaged, defective or incorrect products, we may request photographs or a continuous unboxing video showing the package and the product received. This helps us investigate the issue fairly.

Important:

Please do not throw away the product or packaging until the issue has been reviewed.

5. Custom Pencil Sketches

Customised artwork is created specifically according to the customer's approved requirements. Therefore, cancellation, return or refund requests for custom artwork may be subject to different conditions. These conditions will be communicated before the order is confirmed.

6. Refunds

If a refund is approved, the applicable refund amount and refund method will be communicated to the customer. Refund processing time may depend on the payment method used.

7. Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made as early as possible. Once a customised artwork has been started or an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.

8. Contact Us

For return or refund questions, please contact us through WhatsApp or Email using the Contact page.
