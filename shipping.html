Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: September 2026

1. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may vary depending on the customer's location, product size, weight and delivery service.

The applicable delivery charge will be communicated to the customer before the order is confirmed.

2. Order Confirmation

An enquiry through WhatsApp or email does not automatically confirm an order.

Before confirmation, we will communicate the product details, availability, product price, delivery charge and final order amount.

3. Processing Time

Processing time may vary depending on the product and current availability.

Customised pencil sketches may require additional preparation time. The expected timeframe will be communicated before confirmation.

4. Delivery Time

Delivery time depends on the destination and the delivery service used. Any estimated delivery timeframe provided is an estimate and may be affected by circumstances outside our control.

5. Customer Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact details.

6. Delivery Issues

If a parcel is delayed, damaged or appears to have another delivery issue, please contact us as soon as possible with the relevant order details.

Important:

Please check the final order summary, product details and delivery charges before making payment.

7. Contact

For shipping questions, please contact us through the Contact page.
