Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Receive

When you contact us about an order or enquiry, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and product requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information provided by customers only when reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, process orders, arrange delivery and communicate about requested products or customised artwork.

3. Custom Artwork Photos

If you request a customised pencil sketch, you may send us a photograph for the purpose of creating your artwork. Please send only photographs that you have permission to share with us.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell or rent customer personal information. Information may be shared with delivery or service providers only when necessary to fulfil an order.

5. Payments

Payment arrangements will be communicated directly with the customer after the product, price, delivery charges and order details are confirmed.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services in the future, such as analytics, advertising or social media features. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if required by features or third-party services. Additional information will be provided if such technologies are introduced.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Contact page.
